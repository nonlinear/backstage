# Sprezzatura - Effortless Elegance

## Question
Does complex work appear simple?

## Etymology
Italian Renaissance (Baldassare Castiglione, *The Book of the Courtier*)

## Principle
**Precision that looks casual. Grace under pressure.**

## Applied Rules
- ✅ **Complex work hidden** (extensive checks behind the scenes)
- ✅ **Microanimations feel effortless** (carefully choreographed)
- ✅ **DRY at user level** (minimal gestures, maximum meaning)
- ✅ **Polished result** (no visible strain)

## Examples

### ✅ PASS
- Website microanimations (feel natural, are calculated)
- DRY products (one gesture = many meanings understood)
- Backstage checks (rigorous behind scenes, clean output)
- "The best design is invisible" (Don Norman)

### ❌ FAIL
- Visible complexity (user sees messy internals)
- Clunky animations (obvious effort)
- Verbose UI (repeating instructions)
- Exposed strain (errors surface without grace)

## Philosophy
**"La bella figura senza fatica apparente"** - beauty without apparent effort

## When to Apply
- Animation design
- Error handling
- Interface simplification
- Feature proposals (how much work shown?)
- Process transparency decisions

## Severity
**Medium** - Aesthetic principle (not functional, but identity-defining)
