# Product Tiers (Quality Signal)

**Problem:** How do we show experimental work without misleading users?

**Solution:** Explicit tier system (gestalt-based visual distinction)

## Tier 1: Flagship
- **Signal:** Maximum attention, full branding, detailed docs
- **Stability:** Production-ready or actively developed with commitment
- **Examples:** Site itself, Agregore, Backstage
- **CTA:** "Use", "Contribute", "Translate"

## Tier 2: Experiments
- **Signal:** Documented but unstable, disclaimer visible
- **Stability:** Take at your own risk
- **Examples:** Proof-of-concept tools, research prototypes
- **CTA:** "Test", "Give Feedback"

## Tier 3: Ideas
- **Signal:** Backstage only, minimal visibility
- **Stability:** Ideation phase, not yet built
- **Examples:** Epic notes, research sketches
- **CTA:** "Follow Progress"

**Philosophy:** Honesty > hype. User knows stability at a glance.
