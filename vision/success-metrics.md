# Success Metrics

**Not:** Revenue, users, growth rate

**Yes:**
- **Quality plateau** - Checks stabilize, approval rate >90% → autopilot
- **Commons contribution** - Tools used, cited, forked
- **Grant alignment** - Epics funded by aligned institutions
- **Volunteer engagement** - People contribute skills willingly
- **Aesthetic coherence** - Products feel like they belong together
