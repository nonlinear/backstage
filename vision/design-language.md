# Design Language (Cross-Product)

**Typography:** Serif (flagship elegance)  
**Animation:** Microinteractions (gestalt, wayfinding)  
**Color:** Minimal palette, tier-based (flagship = full, experiments = muted)  
**Layout:** Editorial (research lab + atelier hybrid)  
**Tone:** Direct, honest, warm  

## Inspiration
- MIT Media Lab (rigor)
- Maison Margiela (authorship)
- Vercel (dev culture)
