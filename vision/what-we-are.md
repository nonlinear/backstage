# What We Are

**Nonlinear Studio** is a **synthetic company** - a research atelier where AI agents work as specialists within deterministic, auditable workflows.

We produce **artifacts for the commons** - tools, systems, research - built with:
- **Rigor** (quality checks, version control, transparent process)
- **Elegance** (sprezzatura, minimal gestures, maximal impact)
- **Honesty** (tier system shows stability, no false promises)

**Not** a traditional agency. **Not** a startup chasing growth.

**We are:** A laboratory for nonlinear creation.
