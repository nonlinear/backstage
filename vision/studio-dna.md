# Studio DNA (What Makes Us Unique)

## 1. Process Transparency
- Every decision documented (git commits, epic notes, checks)
- Auditable from idea → delivery
- Backstage visible (behind the scenes is the product)

## 2. Synthetic Company Architecture
- AI agents as domain specialists
- Deterministic + probabilistic checks
- Pipelines define quality gates
- Human vision > efficiency loops

## 3. Commons-Oriented
- Open source by default
- Documentation as product
- Collaborative ethos (grants, volunteers, co-creation)

## 4. Elegant Infrastructure
- Self-hosted (own your tools)
- Minimal but robust
- DRY at every level (code, design, process)
