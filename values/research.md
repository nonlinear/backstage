# Research Before Internal Ideas

## Principle
**Look outward before looking inward. Silent stakeholders exist.**

> Research is epistemological anti-drift

## Philosophy (Epistemological)
**We don't know everything. Environment contains truths we haven't discovered.**

## Rule
**Distrust internal ideas until corroborated externally.**

## Research Types (Context-Dependent)

### 1. Book Research
- Foundational texts
- Seminal works
- Domain expertise

### 2. Stakeholder Research
- Users, collaborators
- Domain experts
- Community feedback

### 3. Landscape Research
- Similar tools/products
- Industry standards
- Best practices

### 4. Internal Logs Research
- Past decisions (git history)
- Check results
- Epic notes
- Retros

## Pattern
1. **Idea emerges** internally
2. **Pause** - check external sources
3. **Compare** - does this align? contradict? novel?
4. **Decide** - informed by research, not just intuition

## Examples

### ✅ RESEARCH FIRST
- Before building feature → check if industry standard exists (Playwright vs custom browser testing)
- Before naming concept → search foundational texts (gestalt, sprezzatura, wayfinding)
- Excited about idea → validate against similar projects

### ❌ INTERNAL ONLY
- "Let's build custom solution!" (without checking if standard exists)
- "This is the right name!" (without researching etymology/usage)
- Assume novelty without landscape scan

## When to Apply
- Before proposing new features
- Before naming concepts
- When overly excited (enthusiasm = bias risk)
- Before architectural decisions
- Case-by-case (varies by project/discipline)

## Philosophy
**"Compare ideas against reality, not just internal logic."**
