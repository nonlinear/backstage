## 💡 KEY STRATEGIES (from books)

### **Debt Payoff:**
1. **Avalanche method:** Pay highest APR first
2. **Snowball method:** Pay smallest balance first (psychological wins)
3. **Balance transfer hacks:** 0% intro APR cards
4. **Negotiate settlements:** 40-60% reduction possible!
5. **Section 609 dispute letters:** Remove errors from credit report

### **Fraud Prevention:**
1. **Chip > swipe** (EMV chips nearly impossible to clone)
2. **Monitor statements weekly** (catch fraud early)
3. **Virtual card numbers** (for online shopping)
4. **Credit freeze** (prevents new accounts)
5. **Report fraud <60 days** (TILA protection!)

### **Legal Rights:**
- **Fair Credit Billing Act:** Dispute charges, max $50 liability
- **Fair Debt Collection Practices Act:** Stop harassment
- **Section 609:** Demand proof of debt (often gets removed!)
