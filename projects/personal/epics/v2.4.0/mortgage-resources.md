## 📚 Books to Download (Anna's Archive)

### 1. **"Mortgage Free!"** - Rob Roy
**Link:** https://annas-archive.li/md5/07c4c88237a565389e70a3a8e4502416
**Format:** EPUB, 14.9MB
**About:** Complete guide to strategies for owning land/home free and clear, without the bank. Escape routes from lifetime debt, long-term financial decisions, avoiding modern-day serfdom.

### 2. **"Save Thousands on Your Mortgage!"**
**Link:** https://annas-archive.li/md5/00fc7fecbeca0a586215d68f72a8411b
**Format:** PDF, 9.2MB (1990)
**About:** Cutting years off mortgage, saving hundreds of thousands in interest, for just a few extra bucks/month. Quick, straightforward, legal strategies.

### 3. **"Mortgage-Backed Securities Workbook"**
**Link:** https://annas-archive.li/md5/f109194e722ba1abf079f4727c37bc11
**Format:** PDF, 11.7MB (1996)
**About:** Calculate MBS prepayment rates, combine analytical techniques, understand scenario/binomial analysis. Technical but powerful.

## 💡 Key Strategies Mentioned in Books

### From "Save Thousands on Your Mortgage"
1. **Bi-weekly payment method** (13 payments/year)
2. **Round-up strategy** (pay extra $50-100/month)
3. **Annual lump sum** (tax refund → principal)
4. **Recast after windfall** (lower payment, same term)

### From "Mortgage Free!"
1. **Debt-free land acquisition first**
2. **Build incrementally** (avoid big loans)
3. **Sweat equity** (DIY to reduce costs)
4. **Long-term perspective** (avoid 30-year trap)
