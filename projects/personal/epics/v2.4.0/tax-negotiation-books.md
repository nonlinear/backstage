## 📚 Recommended Books to Find

### Top Priority: OIC & Tax Negotiation

1. **"Stand Up to the IRS"** by Frederick W. Daily (Nolo Press)
   - Comprehensive IRS negotiation guide
   - OIC strategies and forms
   - Payment plan alternatives
   - **Search:** annas-archive.org/search?q=stand+up+to+the+irs+daily

2. **"The IRS Problem Solver"** by Daniel J. Pilla
   - Tax resolution expert
   - Case studies and strategies
   - **Search:** annas-archive.org/search?q=irs+problem+solver+pilla

3. **"How to Settle with the IRS for Pennies on the Dollar"** by Arnold S. Goldstein
   - Practical OIC guide
   - Negotiation tactics
   - **Search:** annas-archive.org/search?q=settle+irs+pennies+goldstein

4. **"Taxation Without Representation"** (various authors)
   - State tax negotiation (NYS included)
   - **Search:** annas-archive.org/search?q=state+tax+negotiation

5. **"Beat the IRS: Tax Tips for Serious Savers"** by various
   - Legal strategies
   - Debt reduction
   - **Search:** annas-archive.org/search?q=beat+irs+tax+tips
